# Power Glitch

ACME SecureSign appliances have protected sensitive transactions for years. Their hardware-backed signing engine was designed to withstand tampering, side-channel attacks, and physical intrusion.

Last month, a routine maintenance report mentioned an unusual event. During a brief power fluctuation, one signing unit produced an output that failed internal validation. The incident was dismissed as a transient hardware anomaly and the affected signature was archived alongside the others.

Engineers insist the private material never left the device.

Auditors are less convinced.

Your team has obtained a public certificate, a collection of signatures generated by the appliance, and an encrypted archive recovered from the same system.

Somewhere in the noise lies the answer.

*"When two craftsmen build the same structure from opposite ends, even the smallest crack may reveal which brick shifted."*
